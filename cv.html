Jan 2024 – Present

Ph.D. Researcher

Concordia University • Montreal, Canada
  • Google-funded projects: Engineered an early-warning system for common hand-tracking failures in VR, which detects tracking loss before it occurs and warns users with visual, auditory, or haptic feedback. Used eye tracking and user behavior logging to analyze reactions to the warnings, and studied user trust and confidence under false warnings. Resulted in five papers: three as first author (PLOS ONE; IEEE Access; under review at IEEE VR 2027) and two as co-author (IEEE TVCG, ISMAR 2026 Special Issue; under review at IEEE TVCG).
  • Mid-air object manipulation: Designed and validated Object Speed Control with a signed distance field to improve precision in distant mid-air manipulation (IEEE ISMAR 2024).
  • Cross-reality 3D modeling: Built MAR, a cross-reality 3D modeling system that pairs desktop Blender with AR inspection and gesture-based selection, and evaluated it with 20 participants (IEEE TVCG, ISMAR 2026 Special Issue).
  • Cross-reality selection: Designed a mouse-guided snapping technique for selecting vertices, edges, and faces in AR, and evaluated it with 24 participants (in preparation for IJHCI).
  • AI-assisted 3D modeling: Built a cross-reality modeling assistant that lets users refer to a part of a shape by describing, selecting, or drawing on it, and evaluated it with 24 participants (in preparation for IJHCI).
  • AI-assisted authoring in XR: Designed an AR second-screen system for LLM-assisted desktop workflows and quantified the cost of crossing between physical and virtual workspaces (ACM SUI 2026).
  • Developed experimental testbeds in Unity (C#) and conducted mixed-methods user studies for each project.
